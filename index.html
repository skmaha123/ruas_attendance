Student Attendance & Learning Dashboard

Spot fast and slow learners, plan counselling and parent notifications, and see each class at a glance · 🔒 data stays in your browser

How it works

  1. Upload the anonymised Excel template (Attendance_Data sheet), or your own data with the same headers.
  2. Adjust the rules (attendance and score thresholds) to RUAS policy. Every chart updates instantly.
  3. Read the graphs: ★ fast learners, ▲ slow learners, ◆ counselling, ⚑ notify parents.
  4. Open the action lists, review each student and export the decisions to Excel.
⚙ Decision rules (edit to match RUAS policy; loaded from the workbook’s Settings sheet)

Per student: averages across their courses. Counselling also triggers on attendance shortage in 2+ courses, slow learner on low score in 2+ courses, and parent notification on 2+ courses below the notification level.
